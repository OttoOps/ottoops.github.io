# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web (static GitHub Pages, legacy build from `master` root; `CNAME` = ottoops.com)

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and a small amount of vanilla JS. No build step. One Python
script (`scripts/github_snapshot.py`) refreshes the GitHub contributions snapshot
and is run by hand; its output is committed.

## Users

Primary: recruiters, hiring managers and engineering peers evaluating Paul Otto -
reading his story, resume and GitHub activity. Secondary: Meal Planner users and
app-store reviewers (Google Play treats ottoops.com as the Otto Ops LLC
organization website) who need the legal name, contact and privacy links.

## Product Purpose

ottoops.com is the home of Otto Ops LLC, Paul Otto's company, and his personal
professional page. Success: a peer leaves knowing what Paul builds now (data, AI
and platform engineering), what he has shipped (Meal Planner, Habit Quest in
development, ClearBridge), the shape of a 30-year career, and how to reach him.

## Brand Personality

Hands-on, precise, unpretentious. An engineer who runs the systems he builds.
Confident without hype; specifics over adjectives.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages, gradient blobs, glassmorphism.
- Breathless resume copy ("crushing", "pristine", "revolutionary").
- Fake terminal / hacker aesthetics.
