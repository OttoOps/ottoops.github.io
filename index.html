Apps from Otto Ops

Meal Planner

Live on Android and iOS

Meal Planner is for households that plan dinners together. You lay out the week, pull from a recipe library the whole household shares, and the plan becomes a grocery list that anyone in the house can check off at the store.

  • A week plan with navigation across multiple weeks
  • A shared recipe library with tags and privacy controls, and Claude to parse a recipe you paste in
  • A grocery list built from the plan and checked against your pantry, so you skip what you already have
  • Household groups with invites and an avatar for each member

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android, SwiftUI and SwiftData on iOS, Firebase behind both.

Habit Quest

Early development

Habit Quest is a habit tracker built as an RPG. Each habit is a quest with a difficulty tier, finishing one pays out XP, gold and loot, and guilds take on raid quests together. You spend the gold in shops on a world map, like Gnome Depot and Flasks 5th Ave.

A React PWA plus an Expo app for phones, sharing the same game logic.

It's early, so expect rough edges.

About me

I wrote my first paid code in 1992, as a teenager at EG&G. From 1996 to 2003 I served in the US Army as an Armament Repair Section Sergeant.

After the Army I worked my way up the stack. I was an Oracle DBA at Qwest, a database engineer at Beatport, and a MySQL engineer at GoDaddy, where I supported MySQL on more than 2,400 high-traffic servers. Then I architected infrastructure and data services at TrackVia, ran my own consultancy, and walked Ticketmaster's teams from their own data centers to EC2, then to Kubernetes, then to EKS.

Because I've usually been the one paged when my systems break, I judge a design by whether a tired person can recover it at 3am. I've mentored military members, government civilians and contractors, and I count that as part of the job.

I live in Aurora, Colorado, and I've worked remotely for about ten years.
